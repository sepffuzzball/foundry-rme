# Spears

A feature in every military in history, spears have an efficiency unmatched in terms of production, training, and application. It's hard to compete with the simple design, extended reach, and stopping power of a spear. experts have acquired the lunge, and can trade accuracy for a flurry of shots.

## Feat: Spear expert
Choose 4 spears to gain expert training when you take this feat.
+1 damage die when you score a critical hit.
All spears gain the Lunge property, and you can Lunge without using your movement.
Once on your turn, if you have advantage, you can sacrifice it for that roll to make an additional attack against that target, as part of the same action. Any remaining attacks still have advantage.

##### Godendag

_Bludgeoning or Piercing, 5 lbs, 12 gp_

A short spear with a heavy ball or weight to hold the spear point, making use of sweeping bludgeons and weighted piercing attacks. The expert makes full use of the added weight for powerful blows that set their enemy off balance.

Untrained Awkward, Two-Handed, Melee d6, Thrown (10/40), Punch, Puncture, Stagger

Proficient Two-Handed, Melee d6, Thrown (20/60), Punch, Puncture, , Stagger, Trip

Expert Two-Handed, Melee d8, Thrown (30/120), Disarm, Punch, Puncture, Stagger, Trip

Expert Perk
If you score a mighty blow, the next attack against this target gains advantage.

##### Harpoon

_Piercing, 6 lbs, 11 gp_

A barbed throwing spear attached to a 50ft length of silk rope so the target can be pulled toward you. On a hit, your enemy must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be impaled, while a critical hit always impales. A expert of the harpoon impales with ease and can swiftly reel in a harpooned prey.

As a bonus action, make a contested Athletics check against the impaled creature. On a success, you drag it toward you up to a number of feet equal to your Strength score. On its turn, it follows this same rule to pull away from you. Creatures two or more size categories larger than you cause you to move with them while you hold the rope. 

While impaled, the enemy suffers the Wound property. The creature can use its action to make a Strength saving throw, pulling the weapon free on a success.

UntrainedAwkward, One-Handed, Melee d6, Thrown (10/40), Wound

Proficient One-Handed, Melee d6, Thrown (20/60), Barbed, Puncture, Wound

Expert One-Handed, Light, Melee d8, Thrown (30/120), Barbed, Punch, Puncture, Wound

Expert Perk
You drag an enemy toward you a number of feet equal to twice your Strength score, and your target suffers disadvantage to Strength saving throws while it is impaled.

##### Hand Spear

_Piercing, 1 lbs, 2 gp_

A dagger-length hand spear called a siangham or emeici, it is similar in appearance to an arrow with a handle but which has a stabbing point on both ends. The expert uses this balanced weapon to attack in quick succession in close melee.

Untrained One-Handed, Finesse, Light, Melee d4, Thrown (20/40), Puncture, Slip

Proficient One-Handed, Finesse, Light, Melee d4, Thrown (20/40) Balanced, Puncture, Slip

Expert One-Handed, Finesse, Light, Melee d4, Thrown (20/60) Balanced, Nimble, Puncture, Slip

Expert Perk
If you make a second attack on your turn against the same creature, that attack has advantage and you ignore all sources of disadvantage for that attack.

##### Javelin

_Piercing, 1 lb, 1 gp_

A light throwing spear still sturdy enough to be used in melee. A expert can make an Olympian throw, forcing the enemy to pry the javelin free or suffer a major flaw in defense.

UntrainedOne-Handed, Finesse, Melee d6, Thrown (20/60), Punch

Proficient One-Handed, Finesse, Light, Melee d6, Thrown (30/120), Punch

Expert One-Handed, Finesse, Light, Melee d8, Thrown (30/240), Punch, Sunder

Expert Perk
When you score a mighty blow, the target suffers a -2 AC penalty. On its turn an enemy can use an action to succeed on a Strength saving throw to pry the javelin free, destroying it in the process.

##### Lance

_Piercing, 6 lbs, 10 gp_

Designed to unseat mounted enemies, while on foot a lance imposes disadvantage against targets within 5ft, but while mounted gains One-Handed and Punch. experts leverage their mount's strength behind each blow.

Untrained Awkward, Two-Handed, Reach, Melee d8, Brace

Proficient Two-Handed, Reach, Melee d12, Brace

Expert One-Handed, Reach, Melee 2d8, Brace, Stagger

Expert Perk
You can attempt a Shove as a bonus action. While mounted, after you successfully hit a target, you can attempt a shove as part of the same attack and gain advantage in the roll. Additionally, you no longer suffer disadvantage to attack rolls when you use this weapon on foot.

##### Pike

_Piercing, 8 lbs, 5 gp_

An 8-12 ft polearm designed for soldiers in a phalanx against an opposing line. A expert with the pike fights as a phalanx soldier, striking so deftly they can stop a charge.

Untrained Two-Handed, Heavy, Reach, Melee d10, Brace, Puncture

Proficient Two-Handed, Heavy, Reach, Melee d12, Brace, Lunge, Puncture

Expert Two-Handed, Heavy, Reach, Melee 2d8, Brace, Lunge, Punch, Puncture

Expert Perk
You ignore half cover. Additionally, if you hit with an attack on a target more than 5 ft away, it cannot move closer to you until the end of its next turn.

##### Ravenbeak

_Piercing or Slashing, 3 lbs, 15 gp_

This pike has a crescent blade on the side of the tip used to hook or slash, at the cost of penetrative power. The expert can redirect attacks and disarm with ease.

Untrained Two-Handed, Heavy, Reach, Melee d8, Brace, Trip

Proficient Two-Handed, Heavy, Reach, Melee d10, Brace, Entangle, Lunge, Trip

Expert Two-Handed, Heavy, Reach, Melee d12, Brace, Disarm, Entangle, Lunge, Trip

Expert Perk
If an enemy misses you with a melee attack, as a reaction you can cause it to target a creature of your choosing within its reach with that same attack roll.

##### Trident

_Piercing, 3 lbs, 10 gp_

The war trident has sharpened, thicker tines and is used as an axe just as often as a spear. The expert can trap, bind and disarm enemies with ease.

Untrained Awkward, One-Handed, Melee d8, Wound

Proficient One-Handed, Melee d8, Thrown (20/60), Versatile 2d4, Disarm, Wound

Expert One-Handed, Melee d12, Thrown (30/120), Versatile 3d4, Deflect, Disarm, Wound

Expert Perk
You can Deflect attacks against any target in reach. When you wield a trident with two hands, your enemy must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be disarmed when you Deflect.

##### War Spear

_Piercing, 3 lbs, 25 gp_

A short, throw-balanced, banded spear with a hefty counterweight for one-handed control or a two-handed bludgeon. A expert is a true war fighter, using spear techniques as ancient as the armies that employed them.

Untrained Awkward, Two-Handed, Melee d8

Proficient One-Handed, Melee d8, Thrown (20/60), Versatile d10, Double Ended d4, Punch, Puncture

Expert One-Handed, Melee d10, Thrown (30/120), Versatile d12, Double Ended d4, Balanced, Punch, Puncture

Expert Perk
If you use this weapon one-handed, you can use Double Ended as if you were using it with two. In two hands, you deal an extra d4 weapon damage each time you hit with the main melee attack.

##### Winged Spear

_Piercing, 1.5 lbs, 25 gp_

Small protrusions on either side of the spear head prevents a skewered target from closing on its wielder. A expert keeps foes at bay while controlling their position.

Untrained One-Handed, Finesse, Melee d6, Thrown (20/60), Versatile d8

Proficient One-Handed, Finesse, Melee d6, Thrown (30/120), Versatile d8, Disarm, Punc, Riposte

Expert One-Handed, Finesse, Melee d8, Thrown (30/120), Versatile d10, Disarm, Nimble, Punch, Riposte

Expert Perk
If you hit with an attack, you can move your target 5 ft in a direction of your choice, and it can't move closer to you until the start of your next turn.
