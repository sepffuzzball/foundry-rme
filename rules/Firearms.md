# Firearms

These are early industrial period firearms which use cartridges filled with primer, powder, and a projectile that is launched when struck by a hammer or striker. They are prone to jamming, require specialized training, and create loud sound and smoke which can be to the detriment of the user. However, even combat blades struggle to have as much stopping power as even the smallest firearm. Expert firearms wielders can quickly clear a jam, use the firearm as a melee weapon in close combat, and can fire more accurately with their shots.

## Feat: Firearm expert

Choose 4 firearms to gain expert training when you take this feat.
You dry or clear a jam from a Firearm as a bonus action.
All firearm weapons critically hit on at least a 19 or 20, and if you have advantage and both dice would hit, your hit counts as a critical hit.
When you Reload, as a bonus action, you can make an attack with any weapon you are wielding, not just firearms.

##### Bolt-Action Rifle

_Piercing, 12 lbs, 1000 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this cartridge-fed integral-tube full-length rifle has a single barrel and fires a very large projectile with tons of powder and extra stopping force, though it tends to be unwieldy and a bit more difficult to use in close quarters.

Untrained Awkward, Firearm (2), Heavy, Loading (4), Penetrate, Puncture, Ranged 1d12 (100/500), Reload (action), Sighted, Two-Handed, Unwieldy (50)

Basic Firearm (2), Heavy, Loading (4), Penetrate, Puncture, Ranged 2d8 (200/800), Reload (action), Sighted, Two-Handed, Unwieldy (30)

Expert Disarm, Firearm (2), Heavy, Loading (4), Penetrate, Puncture, Ranged 2d8 (250/1000), Reload (Object Interaction (single cartridge)), Sighted, Two-Handed, Unwieldy (30)

Expert Perk
While prone or braced against a structure providing half or three-quarters cover, you may reduce your speed to 0 until the start of your next turn. If you do so, increase your crit range with this weapon to 18-20.

##### Break-Action Revolver

_Piercing, 3 lbs, 1000 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this cartridge-fed break-action single-barreled revolver is loud, powerful, and relatively easy to use if you can acquire the rare ammunition that it requires.

Untrained Firearm (1), Loading (6), Ranged 1d8 (30/90), Reload (Action), Two-Handed

Basic Firearm (1), Loading (6), One-Handed, Ranged 1d8 (30/90), Reload (Action), Versatile (1d10)

Expert Disarm Firearm (1), Loading (6), One-Handed, Ranged 1d10 (30/90), Reload (Bonus), Versatile (1d12)

Expert Perk
While using two hands to wield this weapon, you may expend 3 loaded ammunition instead of the normal 1 as part of an attack. On a hit, in addition to the attack's normal damage the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be knocked prone with their speed halved until the end of their next turn. On a miss, the target must succeed on a Constitution or Dexterity saving throw or have their speed halved until the end of their next turn.

##### Double-Barrelled Shotgun

_Piercing, 8 lbs, 1000 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this cartridge-fed double-barreled shotgun has two independant barrels and fires large, heavy projectiles or lots of small bearing-sized balls.

Untrained Firearm (1), Heavy, Loading (2), Ranged 2d8 (30/60), Reload (Action), Two-Handed

Basic Firearm (1), Heavy, Loading (2), Ranged 2d10 (60/90), Reload (Action), Two-Handed

Expert Firearm (1), Heavy, Loading (2), Ranged 2d10 (60/120), Reload (Attack), Two-Handed

Expert Perk
While the Double-Barelled Shotgun is fully loaded, you can expend 2 cartridges as a single attack to increase the damage to 4d8. Rolling 17, 18, 19, or 20 on the attack roll counts as a critical hit.

##### Hand Cannon

_Piercing, 3 lbs, 1000 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this cartridge-fed triple-rotating-barrel oversized pistol has a much larger barrel closer in size to a larger class of weapon and is thus, much more unwieldy.

Untrained Awkward, Firearm (2), Loading(3), Reload (Action), Two-Handed, Heavy, Penetrate, Ranged 1d10 (30/90), Sighted

Basic Firearm (2), Loading(3), Ranged 1d12 (60/120), Reload (Attack),  Penetrate, Sighted, Steady

Expert One-Handed, Ranged 1d12 (80/320), Reload (Bonus), Firearm, Hipshot, Penetrate, Sighted, Steady

Expert Perk
As an action you may expend 3 loaded ammunition to make a single ranged attack dealing 3d12 damage. The Firearm jam rating increases by 1 until repairs are completed on the Hand Cannon. This penalty can stack, though if the firearm jams at a rating of 5 or more it is destroyed permanently. You may repair the Hand Cannon and reduce the Firearm jam rating by 1 (down to the minimum jam rating) by spending 8 hours (this does not need to be consecutive) with an appropriate set of tools.

##### Lever-Action Rifle

_Piercing, 9 lbs, 1000 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this cartridge-fed integral-tube rifle has a single barrel and fires medium-sized projectiles designed to be used in a variety of situations.

Untrained Firearm (1), Loading (8), Ranged 2d4 (80/160), Reload (Action), Two-Handed, Unwieldy (30)

Basic Firearm (1), Loading (8), Ranged 2d6 (120/320), Reload (Object Interaction (single cartridge)), Two-Handed, Unwieldy (20)

Expert Disarm, Firearm (1), Loading (8), Ranged 2d6 (120/500), Reload (Object Interaction (single cartridge)), Trip, Two-Handed, Unwieldy (10)

Expert Perk
As an action you may expend 3 loaded ammunition to reduce the speed of a creature within your normal range with this weapon to 0 until the end of their next turn.

##### Multi-Purpose Launcher

_Piercing, 8 lbs, 1500 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this large multi-purpose launcher fires oversized cartridges in a slow, lobbing arc and does no direct damage itself, but rather is used to distribute things contained in the cartridges.

Untrained Awkward, Firearm (1), Heavy, Loading (1), Ranged (45/90), Reload (Action), Two-Handed

Basic Firearm (1), Heavy, Loading (1), Ranged (60/120), Reload (Attack), Two-Handed

Expert Firearm (1), Heavy, Loading (1), Ranged (90/150), Reload (Bonus), Two-Handed

Expert Perk
You no longer miss if one of your allies is within the attack radius of attacks with this weapon. Attacks against creatures 2 sizes or more larger than you are made with advantage.

##### Revolving Carbine

_Percing, 6 lbs, 1100 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this cartridge-fed revolving-cylinder shortened rifle has a single barrel and fires a small projectile packing lots of gunpowder to improve penetration properties.

Untrained Firearm (1), Loading (5), Reload (Action), Two-Handed, Penetrate, Ranged 2d4 (100/500)

Basic Firearm (1), Loading (5), Reload (Attack), Two-Handed, Penetrate, Ranged 2d6 (120/600)

Expert Firearm (1), Loading (5), Reload (Bonus), Two-Handed, Penetrate, Ranged 2d6 (120/600)

Expert Perk
As a reaction to a creature moving from outside of your speed to inside of your speed range, you may make an attack with this weapon with disadvantage.

##### Sawed-Off Shotgun

_Piercing, 6 lbs, 1000 gp_

A relic from the Broken World, this cartridge-fed double-barreled shotgun has two independant barrels and fires large, heavy projectiles or lots of small bearing sized balls. It has had much of it's original barrel length removed in an attempt to reduce weight and improve short-range stopping power.

Untrained Firearm (2), Loading (2), Ranged 2d10 (20/45), Reload (Action), Two-Handed

Basic Firearm (2), Loading (2), Ranged 2d12 (30/60), Reload (Action), Two-Handed

Expert Firearm (2), Loading (2), Ranged 2d12 (40/60), Reload (Attack), Two-Handed

Expert Perk
When attacking a creature or object with speed 0 while within this weapon's short range, treat any damage diced rolled with this attack that is below a 10 as a 10.
