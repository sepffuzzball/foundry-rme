# Whips

Whips are a unique weapon, essentially weaponizing a rope in various aspects. The misdirection and increased range of whip weapons leads to a rare fighting style, but one that finds use among more conventional weapons thanks to its increased utility. All whip weapons can be used to aid in exploration, grasping handholds to aid in climbing or swinging. The expert finds the whip crack has increased damage and has levied the grasping utility of these weapons effectively in any scenario.

## Feat: Whip expert

Choose 4 whips to gain expert training when you take this feat
+1 weapon DC
You deal one extra damage dice to targets which are more than 5 ft away from you.
Your Disarm, Entangle, and Trip attacks impose disadvantage on saving throws made to resist them.

##### Kusarigama

_Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing, 4 lbs, 30 gp_

A combination weapon consisting of a spiked ball and kama linked by a long chain. The kama deals slashing and piercing damage, while the ball deals bludgeoning and piercing damage.

Untrained Awkward, Two-Handed, Finesse, Melee d6

Proficient Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d6, Double Ended d6, Entangle, Stagger, Trip

Expert Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d8, Double Ended d8, Entangle, Keen, Stagger, Trip

Expert Perk
If you hit with a main attack and Double Ended attack on the same target on the same turn, you can move your target 5 ft in a direction of your choosing.

##### Meteor Hammer

_Bludgeoning, 3 lbs, 5 gp_

A small weight attached to a rope or chain, used like a rope dart to make circular and explosive lunging attacks. A expert can twirl the weapon around them with varying angles to strike any foe who might get near.

Untrained Awkward, Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d4, Conceal

Proficient Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d4, Conceal, Entangle, Lunge, Trip

Expert Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d4, Conceal, Disarm, Entangle, Lunge, Trip

Expert Perk
As a reaction you can enter a readied stance. Until the start of your next turn, you can make an attack against any creature that enters your reach, and doing so does not use your reaction.

##### Scorpion Whip

_Piercing and Slashing, 2 lbs, 5 gp_

A whip with a small thin blade at the tip. The expert flows the tip effortlessly between targets, keeping the scorpion tip dancing about with unpredictability.

Untrained Awkward, One-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d6

Proficient One-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d6, Entangle, Keen, Slip, Trip

Expert One-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d6, Entangle, Keen, Puncture, Slip, Trip

Expert Perk

If you choose a different target than your previous attack, the attack roll gains +2.

##### Scourge

_Slashing, 3 lbs, 3 gp_

A torture weapon sometimes called a cat-o-nine-tails, made of many whip tails gathered into a bunch. The expert has learned to bleed their enemies with this torture device.

Untrained Awkward, One-Handed, Melee d4, Wound

Proficient One-Handed, Melee d6, Disarm, Trip, Wound

Expert One-Handed, Melee d8, Disarm, Entangle, Lunge, Trip, Wound

Expert Perk
This weapon adds two extra damage dice when it scores a critical hit. When an enemy is affected by the Wound property, it takes two damage dice.

##### Spiked Chain

_Piercing and Slashing, 5 lbs, 10 gp_ 

A length of thin chain, each link given spikes or barbs, used to lash and rip at targets. The expert builds up momentum from missed attack to ply that speed into the next hit.

Untrained Awkward, One-Handed, Reach, Melee d6, Entangle, Sunder

Proficient One-Handed, Reach, Melee d6, Disarm, Entangle, Sunder, Trip

Expert One-Handed, Reach, Melee d8, Disarm, Entangle, Keen, Sunder, Trip

Expert Perk
When you miss, add another damage die to the next attack that hits. At the end of your turn, any extra built up damage dice are lost.

##### War Poi

_Bludgeoning and Fire, 3 lbs, 15 gp_

A hardened censer built to hold oil and set alight. A war poi must be filled with one use of oil using an action and ignited with an object interaction, which then burns for 1 minute. It can be loaded with other liquids such as poisons or water. The expert learns to spray the burning oil in a flare as the weapon wraps around the target.

Untrained Awkward, Two-Handed, Finesse, Melee d4, Fire d4, Clockwork, Trip

Proficient Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d6, Fire d4, Clockwork, Disarm, Entangle, Knockback, Trip

Expert Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d6, Fire d6, Clockwork, Disarm, Entangle, Knockback, Trip

Expert Perk
When you use a property attack, you always deal the fire damage listed.

##### Whip

_Slashing, 1 lbs, 2 gp_

The whip is treated as a melee weapon with 15ft reach. You can use a whip to grasp unattended objects or handholds, and if possible, you can pull them to you. A expert's whips are an extension of their hands.

Untrained Awkward, One-Handed, Finesse, Melee d2

Proficient One-Handed, Finesse, Reach (15ft), Melee d4, Disarm, Entangle, Trip

Expert One-Handed, Finesse, Light, Reach (15ft), Melee d6, Disarm, Entangle, Keen, Trip

Expert Perk
When you Disarm, you can place the item anywhere in your reach, or use your reaction to catch it. An item that can be grabbed with the whip can be placed anywhere else in your reach.

##### Whip Dagger

_Piercing or slashing, 2 lbs, 15 gp_

A dagger with a ring pommel at the end of a silk ribbon. The weapon can be used like a dagger in hand to attack creatures up to 5 ft away, or swung about the end of the ribbon to attack creatures up to 10ft away. A expert can perform the Scorpion Pull, an ability to impale a target and pull them closer with the attached ribbon.

Untrained Awkward, Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d4, Conceal

Proficient Two-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d4, Conceal, Entangle

Expert Two-Handed, Affixed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d4, Conceal, Entangle, Lunge

Expert Perk
When your attack is successful, as a bonus action, you can force the enemy to succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 10ft toward you or prone. You can only do this if your target is no more than one size category larger than you.

##### Whip Sword

_Piercing or Slashing, 3 lbs, 250 gp_

A whip sword can be either a sword or a bladed whip. As a bonus action, you change between the forms of your weapon. As a sword, it is similar to a longsword. As a whip, it gains Reach, and a host of utility options. A expert can quickly change between forms as needed to attack enemies near their entangled prey.

Sword Form
Untrained Awkward, One-Handed, Melee d6, Versatile d8, Clockwork

Proficient One-Handed, Finesse, Melee d8, Versatile d10, Clockwork

Expert One-Handed, Finesse, Melee d8, Versatile d10, Clockwork, Lunge, Puncture

Expert Perk
You can change between weapon forms freely. Additionally, when you hit with this weapon, you can deal only your Strength modifier in damage and automatically Entangle your enemy by changing to its whip profile.

Whip Form

Untrained Awkward, One-Handed, Reach, Melee d4, Clockwork

Proficient One-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d6, Barbed, Clockwork, Entangle, Trip

Expert One-Handed, Finesse, Reach, Melee d8, Barbed, Clockwork, Entangle, Keen, Trip

Expert Perk
While an enemy is entangled, you can attack it or any target within 5ft of it. This forgoes the automatic hit granted by the Entangle property, and if this attack hits, it deals weapon damage and your ability modifier.
